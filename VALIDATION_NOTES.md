# Storefront Visual Validation Notes

The desktop and 390 px-wide Arabic RTL captures completed on 12 August 2026 confirm that the new About, Contact, Delivery and Returns, and FAQ routes share the Noir-and-gold storefront system, preserve legible tap targets, and collapse content cards into a single usable mobile column. The shop filters, product card, customer-information cards, FAQ disclosure controls, footer, and floating WhatsApp entry point remained within the viewport.

The visual review also identified that the previously referenced secondary legacy image was unavailable in the rendered application. It was replaced with the verified, existing storefront hero asset rather than leaving a broken or empty image region. Final mobile captures will confirm this substitution alongside the post-build Lighthouse measurements.
